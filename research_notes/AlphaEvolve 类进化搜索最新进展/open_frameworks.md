## Does "vpevolve" exist? What is it?

### Takeaway
Yes. **VPEvolve** is a real project, but it is a domain application and not a general AlphaEvolve-style framework. It is an LLM agent for computational lithography (OPC recipe optimization), arXiv:2609.32473, submitted 2026-09-26, by Tianyi Li, …, Hao Geng. It "self-evolves" through a Skill Bank of measured experience and leaves the LLM frozen. Its repos are tiny (0–3 stars). If the user meant a general program-evolution framework, the likeliest intended names are **OpenEvolve**, **ShinkaEvolve** and **ThetaEvolve**, followed by AlphaEvolve itself and FunSearch.

### Cited Findings
- Paper: "VPEvolve: A Self-Evolving Virtual Process Engineer for Computational Lithography", arXiv:2609.32473, cs.AI, submitted 26 Sep 2026. Authors: Tianyi Li, Wenxuan Dong, Donger Luo, Nan Wang, Yanpeng Chen, Jiaqi Liu, Xinyun Zhang, Hao Geng — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32473), [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.32473)
- Method: a "Virtual Process Engineer" harness gives a **frozen** language model process manuals, layout analysis, recipe editing and evaluation with a commercial tool. A Skill Bank stores measured engineering experience, and the actor retrieves from it before each trial. The target is OPC (optical proximity correction) recipes — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32473)
- Code release `vpevolve/vpevolve_code_and_data` (Apache-2.0, 0 stars, 2 commits): it contains a Pi agent harness, a standalone differentiable optical and resist simulator, and 20 FreePDK45-derived GDS windows (10 Poly, 10 Metal1). The agent pipeline needs a separately licensed **Calibre** installation and litho model. Each case runs 10 optimization rounds and starts with an empty experience store. Ablation arms: "frozen" (no online updates), without domain reflection, without condition-aware revision, and without prospective advice. It uses adapted "ACE" prompt templates and a local Qwen model through vLLM — [GitHub vpevolve_code_and_data](https://github.com/vpevolve/vpevolve_code_and_data), [GitHub True-Litianyi/VPEVOLVE_code](https://github.com/True-Litianyi/VPEVOLVE_code)
- Related repos found on 2026-11-07: True-Litianyi/VPEVOLVE_code (3 stars, Apache-2.0, created 2026-09-26, updated 2026-11-06); True-Litianyi/VPEVOLVE (project website, 1 star); vpevolve/vpevolve (0 stars, created 2026-09-05) — [GitHub True-Litianyi/VPEVOLVE](https://github.com/True-Litianyi/VPEVOLVE)

### Inferences
- VPEvolve is closer to the "experience/skill-library self-evolving agent" line of work (ACE-style context engineering) than to population-based program evolution. Its LLM is frozen and it optimizes recipes, not code populations. It fits the "*Evolve" family only loosely, as a domain application (EDA and lithography).
- The user may have meant VPEvolve specifically if they work in EDA or lithography. Otherwise "vpevolve" is probably a typo or mishearing of OpenEvolve or ShinkaEvolve, the two most-used open AlphaEvolve clones, or of ThetaEvolve. The report should present both readings explicitly.

## Which open frameworks are most capable/active as of late 2026, and how do they compare on sample efficiency and results?

### Takeaway
By adoption, **OpenEvolve** (~7.7k stars, Apache-2.0) leads by a wide margin, followed by the **Darwin Gödel Machine** (~2.5k), **ShinkaEvolve** (~1.5k) and the LLM4AD platforms (~0.8k + 0.7k). On reported results, n=26 circle packing has saturated around **2.6359–2.63598** across ShinkaEvolve, CodeEvolve, ThetaEvolve, AlphaResearch and optimize_anything, all marginally above AlphaEvolve's 2.63586. The differences are at the 1e-4 level and depend on verifier tolerance. ShinkaEvolve (~150 evaluations) and later cost-focused systems (optimize_anything: 63 evaluations for $3.18; FrugalEvo: $0.55–1.68) are the sample-efficiency leaders. A 2026 paper (Gideoni, Risi, Gal) finds that simple baselines match these pipelines when budgets and search spaces are equal.

## What new techniques beyond AlphaEvolve were introduced (RL fine-tuning of the mutator, meta-prompt evolution, cost reduction)?

### Takeaway
Five directions go beyond AlphaEvolve's frozen-ensemble design:
1. **Training the mutator**: EvoTune (DPO interleaved with search) and ThetaEvolve (test-time RL on a single 8B open model, beating AlphaEvolve's n=26 bound).
2. **Sample and cost efficiency**: ShinkaEvolve (novelty rejection sampling, adaptive parent sampling, bandit LLM selection), FrugalEvo, optimize_anything, and Discovery Loop's single-champion loop.
3. **Better idea generation**: DeepEvolve (deep research and retrieval), AlphaResearch (simulated peer-review reward), and GigaEvo (insight generation and lineage).
4. **Scale-up of the unit of evolution**: SATLUTION evolves whole repositories.
5. **Experience/skill banks with frozen LLMs**: VPEvolve.

### Cited Findings
- RL fine-tuning of the mutator: EvoTune alternates evolutionary search with RL training (DPO with KL regularization for diversity) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.05108), [Moonlight](https://www.themoonlight.io/review/algorithm-discovery-with-llms-evolutionary-search-meets-reinforcement-learning)
- Test-time RL: ThetaEvolve notes that AlphaEvolve is "a pure inference system", so its models cannot internalize evolving strategies. ThetaEvolve adds RL during the evolution run on one open model, and RL consistently beats inference-only — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.23473)
- Sample efficiency: ShinkaEvolve's parent sampling, code-novelty rejection sampling and bandit LLM-ensemble selection — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.19349)
- Meta-prompting and crossover: CodeEvolve ("inspiration"-based crossover and meta-prompting, per the paper title and abstract; details not read) — [arXiv HTML v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.14150v4)
- Research-augmented evolution: DeepEvolve's deep research module — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06056v1)
- Peer-review reward for ideas: AlphaResearch — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.08522)
- Insight generation and bidirectional lineage tracking: GigaEvo — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.17592)
- Repository-scale evolution with build and debug agents and formal verification (DRAT): SATLUTION — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.07367v1)
- Cost reduction: optimize_anything (63 evals, $3.18) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.19633); FrugalEvo ($0.55–$1.68) — [deeplearn.org](https://deeplearn.org/arxiv/838611/frugalevo:-towards-cost-aware-llm-guided-program-evolution); Discovery Loop (10 Packomania records for $27.72, no population) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.05093)

### Inferences
- The clearest technical break from AlphaEvolve is weight updates during search (EvoTune, then ThetaEvolve). They show a small open model with RL can match frontier-ensemble results on open problems. That cuts cost and allows full open-source replication.
- In 2026 the trend has moved from "more elaborate population machinery" to "cheaper and simpler loops" (single champion, frugal budgets) and toward agentic or coding-agent integration (Shinka skill packs, SATLUTION, evolution-kernel built on Claude Code/Aider). This is consistent with the simple-baselines critique.

### Gaps
- Meta-prompt evolution (evolving the system prompt and instructions alongside programs) is present in OpenEvolve, AlphaEvolve and CodeEvolve, but no ablation numbers were retrieved.
- No head-to-head study with matched budgets across OpenEvolve, ShinkaEvolve, ThetaEvolve and CodeEvolve was found, apart from CodeEvolve's own comparison and optimize_anything's comparison against OpenEvolve.
